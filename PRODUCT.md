# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Project Sekai and THE iDOLM@STER Shiny Colors fans, primarily on mobile, who want to quickly generate character sticker images with custom text for sharing to Twitter/X, Discord, Line groups, and similar chat platforms.

## Product Purpose

A lightweight, single-page tool for creating custom sticker images: pick a character, type text, adjust font/position/rotation/color/stroke, then copy or download the generated image. Speed and simplicity matter more than deep customization.

## Brand Personality

Pink, cute, usable.

## Anti-references

- Overly minimal or corporate SaaS tool aesthetics.
- Dark, heavy, "gamer" UI.
- Cluttered interfaces with too many visible controls at once.

## Design Principles

1. **Character-first:** the sticker preview is the hero; controls should not compete with it.
2. **Thumb-friendly:** primary actions and the preview should be comfortable on a phone screen.
3. **Cute but calm:** pink accents and soft shapes, but avoid noisy decoration that distracts from the sticker.
4. **Immediate feedback:** every control change should update the preview in real time.
5. **One-handed where possible:** place the most-used controls within easy reach on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirements identified. Default to standard WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard-accessible controls where Radix primitives already provide it.
